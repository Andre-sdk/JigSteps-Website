0.1-Beta [Currently testing]

This is the closed beta build, available only to invited testers via Google Play. It includes planning up to five grind steps across wheel-type and flat-type grinders, jigs, and supports you own, with the exact support distance calculated for each one — plus the Knife Diary, full equipment library, Tormek SE-77 support, and Metric / Imperial units. Found something odd, or have feedback? Get in touch at support@jigsteps.app.
